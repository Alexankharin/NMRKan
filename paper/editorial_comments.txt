Dear Dr. Sheberstov,

Thank you for submitting your manuscript to PRX Intelligence. We
appreciate your interest and the opportunity to consider your work.

We have identified issues with your manuscript that should be addressed.

PRX Intelligence is a highly-selective, fully open-access journal that
aims to publish exceptional work on artificial intelligence and machine
learning that substantially advances the physical sciences and related
fields. We consistently apply rigorous standards when deciding which
papers proceed to review.

While I greatly appreciate the idea and your Ansatz, I am afraid that
your current ms does not meet the academic standards required to publish
in our journal. In particular, I would like to ask you to (a) exemplify
the usefulness of your approach through inclusion of NMR predictions for
some real materials or molecules, and to (b) strengthen the discussion
of your results by providing more context from the literature (currently
you only reference 12 publications, which is not enough) and by
comparing to the state of the art models published in the literature.
If you appropriately address these concerns, our editorial team will
evaluate your paper and decide on the next step. You may resubmit using
the Submission server at https://authors.aps.org/Submissions/. We only
need the PDF file of the manuscript, not the TeX or Word source file or
figure files.
